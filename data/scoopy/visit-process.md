# Scoopy Doog — What happens on a visit

## A typical service day

1. **60-minute "on the way" text** before arrival. The customer does not need to be home, but the yard must be accessible and no aggressive dogs may be left outside.
2. The technician follows a precise route and **walks the yard multiple times**.
3. A **gate photo** is snapped and uploaded after each visit to confirm the gate is securely closed.
4. Equipment (and shoes) are sprayed and cleaned with **kennel-grade disinfectant** between visits.
5. Waste is **double-bagged and placed in the customer's trash can**. If no trash can is available, it is bagged and left neatly at the side of the house.

## Scheduling

- There is no specific arrival time window. Routes are scheduled at midnight the day before service to minimize drive time.
