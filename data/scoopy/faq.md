# Scoopy Doog FAQ

Last updated: November 2026.

## What is Scoopy Doog?

Scoopy Doog is a professional dog waste removal service for home yards in Orange County, California.

## Does the customer need to be home?

No. The yard must be accessible.

## Can the dog stay outside?

Yes, unless the dog is aggressive. Aggressive dogs must be kept indoors. Homes with aggressive dogs left outside may be removed from the route.

## What if there is no trash can?

The technician leaves the double-bagged waste neatly at the side of the house.

## Does Scoopy Doog clean indoors?

No. Only outdoor spaces are cleaned.

## When will the technician arrive?

Routes are scheduled at midnight the day before service. There is no specific arrival time window. The customer gets a 60-minute on-the-way text before arrival.

## Does Scoopy Doog work year-round?

Yes, including winter.
