# Scoopy Doog — Booking and billing

Source: scoopydoog.com/pooper-scooper-service ("OUR PROCESS") and FAQ,
read Nov 8, 2026.

## How booking works

1. Click **Free Quote**, enter your zip code, and complete signup on the
   website. (Or call the office with questions.)
2. Schedule the first cleanup — a Scoopy Doog rep reaches out to schedule
   it and walk the customer through the process.
3. Set up the customer portal.
4. Add a payment method.
5. Enjoy a doo-free yard.

Online booking and signup exist, and there is a customer portal.

## Billing

- All billing occurs on the **1st of each month, before services are
  provided**.
- Automatic payments via bank account or credit/debit card.
- Invoice plus email receipt, also visible in the customer portal.
- If payment is not received before the first cleaning of the month, the
  customer is removed from the schedule.

## No contracts

- No contracts. Start, pause, or cancel anytime via the customer portal or
  by calling the office.

## Hours

- **Monday–Saturday, 7am–6pm.**
