# Scoopy Doog — Pricing

Source: scoopydoog.com FAQ ("How much does it cost…?"), read Nov 8, 2026.
All prices below are the only pricing facts the business publishes. Do not
invent or quote any other price.

## What plans cost

- Plans start as low as **$60 per month**.
- Most clients pay between **$100 and $135 per month**.
- Pricing varies by yard size, service frequency, and number of dogs.

## Exact quote

- The exact price for a specific yard is given through the **Free Quote**
  flow on the website: enter your zip code and complete signup.
- Per-frequency prices (for example weekly, bi-weekly, or one-time visits)
  are **not published**. Never quote a per-frequency price. Direct the
  customer to the Free Quote flow for an exact quote.

## No contracts

- There are **no contracts**. Start, pause, or cancel anytime via the
  customer portal or by calling the office.

## What is NOT offered (do not claim)

- No money-back or satisfaction guarantee has been published. Do not claim one.
- No first-cleanup-free or other promotion has been published. Do not claim one.
