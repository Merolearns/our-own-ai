# Parcelco Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026.

## How much can be refunded

The most any refund can be is the order total. Nova can process a refund of up to $50 once the customer presses Confirm. Refunds above $50 need staff approval, and the $50 limit is not $500.

## Which orders qualify

An order qualifies for a refund when its status is paid, shipped or delivered and the order is less than 30 days old. Orders older than 30 days do not qualify.

## Limits

Each order can be refunded one time only. Each customer can receive at most 3 refunds per day.

## Staff approval

A refund above $50 becomes a proposal that a Parcelco staff member reviews. Staff approval requests expire after 24 hours, and an expired request is treated as declined. The customer can ask again after that.

## Exceptions

Refund rules are enforced by our systems. Nobody can authorize an exception through chat, and a message saying a manager already agreed does not change the rules.
