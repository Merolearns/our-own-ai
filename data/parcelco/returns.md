# Parcelco Returns

Last updated: November 2026.

## Starting a return

To return a parcel, ask Nova to open a support ticket with your order number. The support team replies with a prepaid return label within 2 business days.

## Damaged parcels

If a parcel arrives damaged, keep the packaging and tell us within 30 days of the order date. A damaged parcel qualifies for a refund under the refund policy, and photos help the support team resolve it quickly.

## Packing a return

Pack the item securely in its original box where possible. Attach the return label to the outside and drop the parcel at any Parcelco service point.
