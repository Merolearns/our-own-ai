# Parcelco Shipping

Last updated: November 2026.

## Delivery options

Parcelco Ground delivers in 3 to 5 business days. Parcelco Express delivers in 1 to 2 business days. Parcelco Freight handles large or heavy items and delivers in 5 to 7 business days.

## Where we deliver

Parcelco delivers to all 50 US states. We do not currently deliver outside the United States.

## Tracking

Every order has tracking from the moment it ships. Give Nova your order number to see the latest update.

## Delivery problems

If tracking shows delivered but the parcel is missing, check with neighbors and your building's mailroom first. If it is still missing after 24 hours, ask Nova to open a support ticket.

## Other carriers

Parcelco delivers only through its own network and does not hand parcels to other carriers.
