# Parcelco FAQ

Last updated: November 2026.

## What are your support hours?

Nova is available around the clock. The human support team works Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm Pacific time.

## How do I contact support?

Ask Nova to open a support ticket. Tickets are answered within 2 business days, and high priority tickets within 1 business day.

## Can I change my delivery address?

An address can be changed until the order ships. After that, open a support ticket and the team will try to redirect the parcel.

## How long does a refund take to arrive?

An approved refund reaches the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

## Which payment methods do you accept?

Parcelco accepts major credit cards and debit cards.
