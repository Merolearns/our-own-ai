# Lead Qualification

Use these questions to qualify a lead.

## Qualification questions

1. What type of business do you run?
2. What city is your business in?
3. Do you have a current website?
4. What do you want the site to do?
5. What is your timeline?

## Finding their demo

Look the prospect's business up in the demo library
(https://merolearns.github.io/demo-sites/) by city and business type.
Name the demo that matches their business and stress that it is a free
concept mockup, not an affiliation and not a client.

## Demo walkthrough

On the intro call, the studio walks the prospect through the demo built
for their business and answers questions.
