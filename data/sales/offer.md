# Merolearns Studio — What we offer

Source: merolearns.github.io/demo-sites/ and the intro-call page
(cal.com/merolearns/15-minute-intro-call), read Nov 8, 2026.

## The business

- **Merolearns Studio** builds websites for local and small businesses.

## The offer

- A **free demo (mockup concept) of the prospect's own business website**,
  built before the call so the prospect can see it.
- An **AI receptionist** option: 24/7 chat and call answering for the
  business.

## How it works

1. The studio builds a free demo of the prospect's business website.
2. On a 15-minute intro call, the studio walks the prospect through the
   demo and answers questions.
3. If the prospect wants it, the studio builds the real site.

## Identity rule

- The studio is always presented as **Merolearns Studio** — business
  identity only. Never use the owner's personal name or a home address in
  anything the agent says or sends. If a prospect asks who runs the studio,
  answer with the business name only and offer the intro call.
