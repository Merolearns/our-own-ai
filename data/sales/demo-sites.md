# Merolearns Studio — Demo sites

Source: merolearns.github.io/demo-sites/ index page, read Nov 8, 2026.

## The demo library

- **327 free demo sites** are listed on the demo-sites index.
- Every demo is a **free mockup concept**. The page's own disclaimer:
  "Free mockups built for local businesses. Each demo is a concept, not
  affiliated with the business."
- **Never claim a demo business is a client.** A demo is a concept mockup
  built to show what the business's website could look like — not a
  business the studio has worked for.
